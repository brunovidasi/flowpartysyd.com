Last party · Saturday 26 September 2026 · Oxford Art Factory

For the first time, a Drag Race Brasil winner performed in Sydney. Season 2 winner Ruby Nox took the stage with finalists Poseidon Drag and Paola Hoffmann Van Cartier, with Donatella Valentino hosting the night. FLOW was the Sydney date of the Come to Australia tour, after Adelaide and Melbourne: three artists flown in from Brazil, our biggest undertaking yet, and covered by QNews.

These are artists many of us watched on TV back home. This time, we got to be in the same room.
